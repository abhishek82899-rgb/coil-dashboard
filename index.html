      Scope. This is a mechanical candidate-flagging tool, not a signal or investment advice. Every heuristic here — contraction detection, the RS-ranking proxy, pivot detection — has documented false-positive/negative limitations (see the notes below and the Track Record tab's backtest caveats). Every setup still needs verification on an actual chart before acting on it.

      Methodology

      Trend Template & VCP. Trend Template (8-criteria uptrend confirmation) + a weekly-swing contraction heuristic on DhanHQ daily history (1.5 shrink tolerance -- each pullback leg must shrink by at least a third vs. the one before, approximating wiki/concepts/vcp.md's documented "roughly half" pattern); RS% is a within-universe percentile proxy, not a true IBD ranking. vs Nifty 50 and vs sector are trailing ~1-year return, stock minus benchmark, in percentage points — the sector index is a heuristic match from yfinance's sector/industry classification onto the closest NSE sector index, not an official classification, showing "n/a" where nothing maps cleanly. Fundamentals verdict comes from yfinance earnings/revenue growth vs. a 15%/10% threshold, with growth above 150% flagged as a likely base-effect artifact. Buy/Stop/Target: entry locks to the price when a stock is first tracked; Stop is Minervini's "danger point" — 2% below the most recent VCP contraction's actual low, falling back to a flat 8% if no contraction reference exists. Target is a 2:1 reward:risk reference level, not a fixed profit target — Minervini himself sells into strength or trails a stop instead. Pivot is the peak of the most recent, tightest contraction; pivot/contraction values past a 30% sanity band are suppressed rather than shown.

      Position tracking. The ₹10L fresh-capital sleeve is risk-parity sized (equal ₹ risk per position, the direct reading of position% × stop% = equity at risk) across a data-driven shortlist, not every screened candidate, and not a recommendation. It's ongoing, not a one-off deployment: it recycles itself after every scan — a position that hits its stop is closed at the live price, its capital returns to cash, and that cash redeploys into the next candidate that clears the bar, rather than sitting idle. Selection requires fundamentals_verdict == "strong" (excludes weak/unknown AND "strong_flagged" -- real growth but flagged as an implausible/base-effect magnitude) and positive relative strength vs. the stock's own NSE sector index, and a symbol already tried once (open or closed) in this sleeve is never re-entered; any candidate whose stop came out wider than ~12% is dropped rather than sized, and cash with no qualifying candidate simply waits rather than forcing a marginal pick. Day P&L compares live price against the most recently completed daily close and resets each day; Total P&L is cumulative since entry. Exit-timing badges (next to a symbol) surface three of Minervini's sell-side signals -- informational only, never an auto-exit: Extended (up 25%+ in ~3 weeks or 70%+ in ~2 weeks, his climax-top range), Heavy vol↓ (the heaviest-volume day of the last 20 sessions closed down), and +20%/8wk (O'Neil's rule -- 20%+ within 8 weeks of entry, a hold signal, shown in green rather than amber since it's the opposite of a warning). Base-count and P/E-expansion signals from the same source aren't mechanized here -- they need history this system doesn't track and are judgment calls even in the source material.

      Track Record. A backtest of this exact methodology, not a live-trading record — see that tab's own caveats (survivorship bias, weekly-checkpoint granularity, RS% -only selection) before trusting any number there.
